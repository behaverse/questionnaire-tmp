## Description

The Social Media Disorder Scale (SMD) is a 9-item self-report questionnaire measuring addictive and disordered patterns of social media use, modeled on behavioral addiction criteria. It is used to screen for problematic social media engagement and study its associations with mental health outcomes.

## Instructions

Social media refers to internet/mobile phone sites such as Facebook, YouTube, Twitter, Instagram, as well as to blogs.<br><br> During the past year, have you ...

## Items

1. **regularly found that you can't think of anything else but the moment that you will be able to use social media again?**  _(dimension: yesno)_
   - 1. yes [score: 1] · 2. no [score: 0]
2. **regularly felt dissatisfied because you wanted to spend more time on social media?**  _(dimension: yesno)_
   - 1. yes [score: 1] · 2. no [score: 0]
3. **often felt bad when you could not use social media?**  _(dimension: yesno)_
   - 1. yes [score: 1] · 2. no [score: 0]
4. **tried to spend less time on social media, but failed?**  _(dimension: yesno)_
   - 1. yes [score: 1] · 2. no [score: 0]
5. **regularly neglected other activities (e.g. hobbies, sport) because you wanted to use social media?**  _(dimension: yesno)_
   - 1. yes [score: 1] · 2. no [score: 0]
6. **regularly had arguments with others because of your social media use?**  _(dimension: yesno)_
   - 1. yes [score: 1] · 2. no [score: 0]
7. **regularly lied to your parents or friends about the amount of time you spend on social media?**  _(dimension: yesno)_
   - 1. yes [score: 1] · 2. no [score: 0]
8. **often used social media to escape from negative feelings?**  _(dimension: yesno)_
   - 1. yes [score: 1] · 2. no [score: 0]
9. **had serious conflict with your parents, brother(s) or sister(s) because of your social media use?**  _(dimension: yesno)_
   - 1. yes [score: 1] · 2. no [score: 0]
