## Description

The Primary Care PTSD Screen for DSM-5 (PC-PTSD-5) is a 5-item screen designed to identify individuals with probable PTSD.

## Instructions

Sometimes things happen to people that are unusually or especially frightening, horrible, or traumatic. For example: a serious accident or fire a physical or sexual assault or abuse an earthquake or flood a war seeing someone be killed or seriously injured having a loved one die through homicide or suicide.

## Items

1. **Have you ever experienced this kind of event?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes [score: 1] · 2. No [score: 0]
2. **had nightmares about the event(s) or thought about the event(s) when you did not want to?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes [score: 1] · 2. No [score: 0]
3. **tried hard not to think about the event(s) or went out of your way to avoid situations that reminded you of the event(s)?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes [score: 1] · 2. No [score: 0]
4. **been constantly on guard, watchful, or easily startled?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes [score: 1] · 2. No [score: 0]
5. **felt numb or detached from people, activities, or your surroundings?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes [score: 1] · 2. No [score: 0]
6. **felt guilty or unable to stop blaming yourself or others for the event(s) or any problems the event(s) may have caused?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes [score: 1] · 2. No [score: 0]
