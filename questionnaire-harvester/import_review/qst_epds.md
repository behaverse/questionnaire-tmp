# Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS) (`qst_epds`)

**Original:** https://us.psytoolkit.org/survey-library/depression-epds.html

- short_title: EPDS
- source: psytoolkit.org
- license: unknown
- publication: Cox, J.L., Holden, J.M., and Sagovsky, R. (1987). Detection of
postnatal depression: Development of the 10-item Edinburgh Postnatal
Depression Scale. British Journal of Psychiatry , 150, 782-786. (1987)
- items: 10

## Description

Postnatal
depression (or also known as postpartum depression , PPD) is a type
of depression following childbirth.

## Items

1. **Note, all questions in this questionnaire are about how you felt <b>in the past 7 days.</b><br><br> I have been able to laugh and see the funny side of things**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. As much as I always could [score: 0] · 2. Not quite so much now [score: 1] · 3. Definitely not so much now [score: 2] · 4. Not at all [score: 3]
2. **I have looked forward with enjoyment to things**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. As much as I ever did [score: 0] · 2. Rather less than I used to [score: 1] · 3. Definitely less than I used to [score: 2] · 4. Hardly at all [score: 3]
3. **I have blamed myself unnecessarily when things went wrong**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes, most of the time [score: 3] · 2. Yes, some of the time [score: 2] · 3. Not very often [score: 1] · 4. No, never [score: 0]
4. **I have been anxious or worried for no good reason**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. No, not at all [score: 0] · 2. Hardly ever [score: 1] · 3. Yes, sometimes [score: 2] · 4. Yes, very often [score: 3]
5. **I have felt scared or panicky for no very good reason**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes, quite a lot [score: 3] · 2. Yes, sometimes [score: 2] · 3. No, not much [score: 1] · 4. No, not at all [score: 0]
6. **Things have been getting on top of me**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes, most of the time I haven’t been able to cope at all [score: 3] · 2. Yes, sometimes I haven’t been coping as well as usual [score: 2] · 3. No, most of the time I have coped quite well [score: 1] · 4. No, I have been coping as well as ever [score: 0]
7. **I have been so unhappy that I have had difficulty sleeping**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes, most of the time [score: 3] · 2. Yes, sometimes [score: 2] · 3. Not very often [score: 1] · 4. No, not at all [score: 0]
8. **I have felt sad or miserable**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes, most of the time [score: 3] · 2. Yes, quite often [score: 2] · 3. Not very often [score: 1] · 4. No, not at all [score: 0]
9. **I have been so unhappy that I have been crying**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes, most of the time [score: 3] · 2. Yes, quite often [score: 2] · 3. Only occasionally [score: 1] · 4. No, never [score: 0]
10. **The thought of harming myself has occurred to me**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes, quite often [score: 3] · 2. Sometimes [score: 2] · 3. Hardly ever [score: 1] · 4. Never [score: 0]
