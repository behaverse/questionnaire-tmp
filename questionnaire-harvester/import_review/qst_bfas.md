# Facebook Addition Scale (BFAS) (`qst_bfas`)

**Original:** https://us.psytoolkit.org/survey-library/addiction-bergen-facebook.html

- short_title: BFAS
- source: psytoolkit.org
- license: unknown
- publication: Andreassen, C.S., Torsheim, T., Brunborg, G.S., & Pallesen, S. (2010). Development of a Facebook addiction scale. Psychological Reports, 110 , 2, 501-517. (2010)
- items: 6

## Description

Facebook addiction is a specific form of Internet addiction in which a
person is addicted to checking Facebook. Anyone travelling on public
transportation can observe this phenomenon.

## Instructions

How often during the last year have you ...
