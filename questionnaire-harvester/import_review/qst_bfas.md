# Facebook Addition Scale (BFAS) (`qst_bfas`)

**Original:** https://us.psytoolkit.org/survey-library/addiction-bergen-facebook.html

- short_title: BFAS
- source: psytoolkit.org
- license: unknown
- publication: Andreassen, C.S., Torsheim, T., Brunborg, G.S., & Pallesen, S. (2010). Development of a Facebook addiction scale. Psychological Reports, 110 , 2, 501-517. (2010)
- items: 6

## Instructions

How often during the last year have you ...

## Items

1. **spent a lot of time thinking about Facebook or planned use of Facebook?**  _(dimension: frequency)_
   - 1. very rarely (0) · 2. rarely (0) · 3. sometimes (1) · 4. often (1) · 5. very often (1)
2. **felt an urge to use Facebook more and more?**  _(dimension: frequency)_
   - 1. very rarely (0) · 2. rarely (0) · 3. sometimes (1) · 4. often (1) · 5. very often (1)
3. **used Facebook in order to forget about personal problems?**  _(dimension: frequency)_
   - 1. very rarely (0) · 2. rarely (0) · 3. sometimes (1) · 4. often (1) · 5. very often (1)
4. **tried to cut down on the use of Facebook without success?**  _(dimension: frequency)_
   - 1. very rarely (0) · 2. rarely (0) · 3. sometimes (1) · 4. often (1) · 5. very often (1)
5. **become restless or troubled if you have been prohibited from using Facebook?**  _(dimension: frequency)_
   - 1. very rarely (0) · 2. rarely (0) · 3. sometimes (1) · 4. often (1) · 5. very often (1)
6. **used Facebook so much that it has had a negative impact on your job/studies?**  _(dimension: frequency)_
   - 1. very rarely (0) · 2. rarely (0) · 3. sometimes (1) · 4. often (1) · 5. very often (1)
