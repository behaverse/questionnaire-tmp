## Instructions

Please indicate how often you have felt or behaved in the following ways, <b>during the past two weeks, including today</b>.

## Items

1. **I have been grumpy**  _(dimension: howoften)_
   - 1. Never (1) · 2. Rarely (2) · 3. Sometimes (3) · 4. Often (4) · 5. Very often (5) · 6. Always (6)
2. **I have been feeling like I might snap**  _(dimension: howoften)_
   - 1. Never (1) · 2. Rarely (2) · 3. Sometimes (3) · 4. Often (4) · 5. Very often (5) · 6. Always (6)
3. **Other people have been getting on my nerves**  _(dimension: howoften)_
   - 1. Never (1) · 2. Rarely (2) · 3. Sometimes (3) · 4. Often (4) · 5. Very often (5) · 6. Always (6)
4. **Things have been bothering me more than they normally do**  _(dimension: howoften)_
   - 1. Never (1) · 2. Rarely (2) · 3. Sometimes (3) · 4. Often (4) · 5. Very often (5) · 6. Always (6)
5. **I have been feeling irritable**  _(dimension: howoften)_
