## Description

The Shutdown Dissociation Scale (SHUT-D) is a 13-item measure assessing dissociative responses — including freeze, flight/fight, fright, and flag/faint reactions — triggered by trauma reminders in adults. It is used in clinical trauma research to evaluate the severity of peritraumatic and trauma-related dissociation grounded in the defense cascade model.

## Instructions

There are 13 questions. These questions have been designed for adults and cover experiences within the last 6 months or in the months since the trauma occurred (for people who have experienced only a single trauma that occurred less than 6 month before).

## Items

1. **Have you fainted? Have you been passing out?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
2. **Have you felt dizzy and has your vision gone black? Felt dizzy and couldn’t see anymore, as though you were blind?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
3. **Have you felt as though you couldn’t hear for a while, as though you were deaf? When people were talking to you, did they sound far away?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
4. **Have you had an experience of not being able to properly see things around you (blurred vision for example)?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
5. **Have you felt as though your body or a part of your body has gone numb?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
6. **Have you felt as though you couldn’t move for a while, as though you were paralyzed?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
7. **Have you felt as though your body, or a part of it was insensitive to pain (analgesia)?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
8. **Have you been in a state in which your body suddenly felt heavy and tired?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
9. **Have you experienced that your body becoming stiff for a while?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
10. **Have you felt nauseous? Have you felt as though you were about to throw up? Have you felt yourself break out in a cold sweat?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
11. **Have you had an “out-of-body” sensation? Have you felt as though you were outside of your body?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
12. **Have you had moments in which you have found yourself unable to speak? Have you been able to speak only with great effort? Have you had an experience in which you could only whisper for a period of time?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Not at all [score: 0] · 2. once a week or less [score: 1] · 3. 2-4 time a week [score: 2] · 4. 5 or more times a week [score: 3]
13. **Have you felt suddenly weak and warm?**  _(dimension: rating)_
