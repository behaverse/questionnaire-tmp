## Description

Flourishing means doing really well . The Flourishing Scale
measures people’s success (self rated) in key areas of one’s life,
such as relationships and having a sense of purpose.

## Instructions

Below are 8 statements with which you may agree or disagree. Using scale below, indicate your agreement with each item by indicating that response for each statement.
