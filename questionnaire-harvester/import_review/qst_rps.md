# Risk Propensity Scale (RPS) (`qst_rps`)

**Original:** https://us.psytoolkit.org/survey-library/risk-rps.html

- short_title: RPS
- source: psytoolkit.org
- license: unknown
- publication: Meertens, R. M. & Lion, R. (2008). Measuring an Individual’s
Tendency to Take Risks: The Risk Propensity Scale. Journal of Applied
Social Psychology, 38 (6) , 1506-1520. (2008)
- items: 7

## Description

Some people are more likely to take risks than others. Some people
might even enjoy taking risks.

## Instructions

Please indicate the extent to which you agree or disagree with the following statements by sliding the slider.<br> Please do not think too long before answering; usually your first inclination is also the best one.

## Items

1. **Safety first**  _(dimension: rating; reversed)_
   - number 1–9 (step 1): "totally disagree" … "totally agree" · initial 5
2. **I do not take risks with my health**  _(dimension: rating; reversed)_
   - number 1–9 (step 1): "totally disagree" … "totally agree" · initial 5
3. **I prefer to avoid risks**  _(dimension: rating; reversed)_
   - number 1–9 (step 1): "totally disagree" … "totally agree" · initial 5
4. **I take risks regularly**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - number 1–9 (step 1): "totally disagree" … "totally agree" · initial 5
5. **I really dislike not knowing what is going to happen**  _(dimension: rating; reversed)_
   - number 1–9 (step 1): "totally disagree" … "totally agree" · initial 5
6. **I usually view risks as a challenge**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - number 1–9 (step 1): "totally disagree" … "totally agree" · initial 5
7. **I see myself as a ...**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - number 1–9 (step 1): "risk avoider" … "risk taker" · initial 5
