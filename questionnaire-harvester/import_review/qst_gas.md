## Instructions

How often during the last six months...

## Items

1. **did you think about playing a game all day long?**  _(dimension: howoften)_

2. **did you spend increasing amounts of time on games?**  _(dimension: howoften)_
   - 1. never (0) · 2. rarely (0) · 3. sometimes (1) · 4. often (1) · 5. very often (1)
3. **did you play games to forget about real life?**  _(dimension: howoften)_
   - 1. never (0) · 2. rarely (0) · 3. sometimes (1) · 4. often (1) · 5. very often (1)
4. **have others unsuccessfully tried to reduce your game use?**  _(dimension: howoften)_
   - 1. never (0) · 2. rarely (0) · 3. sometimes (1) · 4. often (1) · 5. very often (1)
5. **have you felt bad when you were unable to play?**  _(dimension: howoften)_
   - 1. never (0) · 2. rarely (0) · 3. sometimes (1) · 4. often (1) · 5. very often (1)
6. **did you have fights with others (e.g., family, friends) over your time spent on games?**  _(dimension: howoften)_
   - 1. never (0) · 2. rarely (0) · 3. sometimes (1) · 4. often (1) · 5. very often (1)
7. **have you neglected other important activities (e.g., school, work, sports) to play games?**  _(dimension: howoften)_
