# Young Mania Rating Scale (YMRS) (`qst_ymrs`)

**Original:** https://psychology-tools.com/test/young-mania-rating-scale

- short_title: YMRS
- source: psychology-tools.com
- license: unknown
- publication: R Young, et al. A Rating Scale for Mania: Reliability, Validity and Sensitivity. 133: Br J Psychiatry 429-435. 1978. (1978)
- items: 11

## Instructions

The YMRS is typically administered by a third-party clinician, but it is provided here, in a slightly reworded form, as a self-assessment. This may not be as accurate when self-administered, as people suffering from mania are often unable to properly assess relevant outward symptoms.

## Items

1. **Elevated Mood**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Absent (0) · 2. Mildly or possibly increased (1) · 3. Definite subjective elevation; optimistic, self-confident; cheerful; appropriate to content (2) · 4. Elevated, inappropriate to content; humorous (3) · 5. Euphoric, inappropriate laughter, singing (4)
2. **Increased Motor Activity or Energy**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Absent (0) · 2. Subjectively increased (1) · 3. Animated; gestures increased (2) · 4. Excessive energy; hyperactive at times; restless (can be calmed) (3) · 5. Motor excitement; continuous hyperactivity (cannot be calmed) (4)
3. **Sexual Interest**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Normal; not increased (0) · 2. Mildly or possibly increased (1) · 3. Definite subjective increase (2) · 4. Spontaneous sexual content; elaborates on sexual matters; hypersexual (3) · 5. Overt sexual acts (4)
4. **Sleep**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. No decrease in sleep (0) · 2. Sleeping less than normal amount by up to one hour (1) · 3. Sleeping less than normal by more than one hour (2) · 4. Decreased need for sleep (3) · 5. No need for sleep at all (4)
5. **Irritability**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Absent (0) · 2. Subjectively increased (2) · 3. Irritable at times; recent episodes of anger or annoyance (4) · 4. Frequently irritable; short, curt (6) · 5. Hostile, uncooperative (8)
6. **Speech: Rate & Amount**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. No increase (0) · 2. Feel talkative (2) · 3. Increased rate or amount at times, verbose at times (4) · 4. Push; consistently increased rate and amount; (6) · 5. Pressured; uninterruptedly, continuous speech (8)
7. **Language: Thought Disorder**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Absent (0) · 2. Circumstantial; mild distractibility; quick thoughts (1) · 3. Distractible; loses goal of thought; change topics frequently; racing thoughts (2) · 4. Flight of ideas; tangentially; difficult to follow; rhyming, echolalia (3) · 5. Incoherent; communication impossible (4)
8. **Content**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Normal (0) · 2. Questionable plans, new interests (2) · 3. Special project(s); hyper religious (4) · 4. Grandiose or paranoid ideas; ideas of reference (6) · 5. Delusions; hallucinations (8)
9. **Disruptive or Aggressive Behavior**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Absent (0) · 2. Sarcastic; loud at times, guarded (2) · 3. Demanding; threats (4) · 4. Threats, shouting (6) · 5. Assaultive; destructive (8)
10. **Appearance**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Appropriate dress and grooming (0) · 2. Minimally unkempt (1) · 3. Poorly groomed; moderately disheveled; overdressed (2) · 4. Disheveled; partly clothed; garish make-up (3) · 5. Completely unkempt; decorated; bizarre garb (4)
11. **Insight**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Present; admits illness; agrees with need for treatment (0) · 2. Possibly ill (1) · 3. Admits behavior change, but denies illness (2) · 4. Admits possible change in behavior, but denies illness (3) · 5. Denies any behavior change (4)
