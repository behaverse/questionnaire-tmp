# Diagnostic Screen for Gambling Disorders (NODS-CLiP) (`qst_nodscl`)

**Original:** https://psychology-tools.com/test/nods-clip

- short_title: NODS-CLiP
- source: psychology-tools.com
- license: unknown
- publication: H Xian, K R Shah, S M Phillips, J F Scherrer, R Volberg, A Eisen. Association of cognitive distortions with problem and pathological gambling in adult male twins. 160(3): Psychiatry Res 300-307. 2008. (2008)
- items: 17

## Instructions

For each question, check YES or NO, taking care to not mark questions you are instructed to skip.

## Items

1. **Have there ever been periods lasting 2 weeks or longer when you spent a lot of time thinking about your gambling experiences, or planning out future gambling ventures or bets?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
2. **Have there ever been periods lasting two weeks or longer when you spend a lot of time thinking about ways of getting money to gamble with?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
3. **Have you ever lied to family members, friends, or others about how much you gamble or how much money you lost on gambling?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
4. **If so, had this happened three or more times?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
5. **Have you ever tried to stop, cut down, or control your gambling?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
6. **On one or more of the times when you tried to stop, cut down, or control your gambling, were you restless or irritable?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
7. **Have you ever tried but not succeeded in stopping, cutting down, or controlling your gambling?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
8. **Has this happened three or more times?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
9. **Have there ever been periods when you needed to gamble with increasing amounts of money or with larger bets than before in order to get the same feeling of excitement?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
10. **Have you ever gambled to relieve uncomfortable feelings such as guilt, anxiety, helplessness, or depression?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
11. **Have you ever gambled as a way to escape from personal problems?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
12. **Has there ever been a period when, if you lost money gambling one day, you would often return another day to get even?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
13. **Have you ever written a bad check or taken money that didn’t belong to you from family members or anyone else in order to pay for your gambling?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
14. **Has your gambling ever caused serious or repeated problems in your relationships with any of your family members or friends?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
15. **Has your gambling caused you any problems in school, such as missing classes or days of school, or your grades dropping?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
16. **Has your gambling ever caused you to lose a job, have trouble with your job, or miss out on an important job or career opportunity?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
17. **Have you ever needed to ask family members or anyone else to loan you money or otherwise bail you out of a desperate money situation that was largely caused by your gambling?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Yes (1) · 2. No (0)
