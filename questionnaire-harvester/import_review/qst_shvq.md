## Description

The Short Hypermasculinity Values Questionnaire (HVQ) is a 16-item self-report scale measuring endorsement of hypermasculine attitudes toward sexuality, violence, and danger. It is used in gender and aggression research to assess masculine ideology as a potential risk factor for interpersonal violence.

## Instructions

Please read each of the following statements carefully. Indicate how much you personally agree or disagree with each one by selecting the number that best represents your view. There are no right or wrong answers—we are interested in your own opinions, not what you think is socially acceptable. Your responses are anonymous and will be used for research purposes only.
