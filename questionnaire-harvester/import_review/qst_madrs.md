# Montgomery-Asberg Depression Rating Scale (MADRS) (`qst_madrs`)

**Original:** https://psychology-tools.com/test/montgomery-asberg-depression-rating-scale

- short_title: MADRS
- source: psychology-tools.com
- license: unknown

## Description

Take the MADRS test to evaluate depression severity, based on well-researched criteria focusing on mood, thoughts, and physical symptoms.

## Instructions

Please note : This test is typically administered by a third-party clinician, but it is provided here informational purposes. This test may not be as accurate when self-administered, as it relies on a somewhat objective assessment.

3. **Inner Tension Representing feelings of ill-defined discomfort, edginess, inner turmoil, mental tension mounting to either panic, dread or anguish. Rate according to intensity, frequency, duration and the extent of reassurance called for.**  _(dimension: rating)_

   - 1. (0) Normal or increased appetite. [score: 0] · 2. (1) [score: 1] · 3. (2) Slightly reduced appetite. [score: 2] · 4. (3) [score: 3] · 5. (4) No appetite. Food is tasteless. [score: 4] · 6. (5) [score: 5] · 7. (6) Needs persuasion to eat at all. [score: 6]
