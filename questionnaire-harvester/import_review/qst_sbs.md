## Description

Supernatural beliefs are
beliefs in things that do not and cannot exist to the best of
scientific knowledge, but which appear real to believers. Examples are
the belief in god, angels, daemons, reincarnation , ghosts, and mind-body
dualism .

## Instructions

Please indicate your agreement with the following statements<br> Note: The scale runs from strongly disagree to strongly agree, you need to choose the level agreement that matches your personal beliefs.<br>
