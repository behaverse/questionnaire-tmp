# Nurturant Fathering Scale (NFS) (`qst_nfs`)

**Original:** https://us.psytoolkit.org/survey-library/nurturant-fathering.html

- short_title: NFS
- source: psytoolkit.org
- license: unknown
- publication: Finley, G.E. & Schwartz, S.J. (2004). The father involvement and nurturant fathering scales: retrospective measures for adolescent and adult children. Educational and Psychological Measurement, 64(1) , 143-164. DOI: 10.1177/0013164403258453. (2004)
- items: 9

## Description

This scale is about the perception of a father’s role in one’s
life. Arguably, the expectations about fathers has changed over the
course of the 20th century ( Finley and Schwartz, 2004 ).

## Items

1. **How much do you think your father <i>enjoyed</i> being a father?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. A great deal [score: 5] · 2. Very much [score: 4] · 3. Somewhat [score: 3] · 4. A little [score: 2] · 5. Not at all [score: 1]
2. **When you needed your father’s <i>support</i>, was he there for you?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Always there for me [score: 5] · 2. Often there for me [score: 4] · 3. Sometimes there for me [score: 3] · 4. Rarely there for me [score: 2] · 5. Never there for me [score: 1]
3. **Did your father have enough <i>energy</i> to meet your needs?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Always [score: 5] · 2. Often [score: 4] · 3. Sometimes [score: 3] · 4. Rarely [score: 2] · 5. Never [score: 1]
4. **Did you feel that you could <i>confide in</i> (talk about important personal things with) your father?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Always [score: 5] · 2. Often [score: 4] · 3. Sometimes [score: 3] · 4. Rarely [score: 2] · 5. Never [score: 1]
5. **Was your father available to spend <i>time</i> with you in activities?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Always [score: 5] · 2. Often [score: 4] · 3. Sometimes [score: 3] · 4. Rarely [score: 2] · 5. Never [score: 1]
6. **How emotionally <i>close</i> were you to your father?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Extremely close [score: 5] · 2. Very close [score: 4] · 3. Somewhat close [score: 3] · 4. A little close [score: 2] · 5. Not at all close [score: 1]
7. **When you were an <i>adolescent</i> (teenager), how well did you get along with your father?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Very well [score: 5] · 2. Well [score: 4] · 3. Ok [score: 3] · 4. Poorly [score: 2] · 5. Very poorly [score: 1]
8. **Overall, how would you <i>rate</i> your father?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Outstanding [score: 5] · 2. Very good [score: 4] · 3. Good [score: 3] · 4. Fair [score: 2] · 5. Poor [score: 1]
9. **As you go through your day, how much of a <i>psychological presence</i> does your father have in your daily thoughts and feelings?**  _(dimension: rating)_
   - 1. Always there [score: 5] · 2. Often there [score: 4] · 3. Sometimes there [score: 3] · 4. Rarely there [score: 2] · 5. Never there [score: 1]
