- items: 3

## Description

The Three Item Loneliness Scale (TILS) is a short scale ideally suited
for studies in which many other questions are asked. This reduces
fatigue of participants. It is based on the longer Revised UCLA scale.

## Instructions

The next questions are about how you feel about different aspects of your life. For each one, select how often you feel that way.

## Items

1. **How often do you feel that you lack companionship?**  _(dimension: tilsscale)_
   - 1. Hardly ever [score: 1] · 2. Some of the time [score: 2] · 3. Often [score: 3]
2. **How often do you feel left out?**  _(dimension: tilsscale)_
   - 1. Hardly ever [score: 1] · 2. Some of the time [score: 2] · 3. Often [score: 3]
3. **How often do you feel isolated from others?**  _(dimension: tilsscale)_
   - 1. Hardly ever [score: 1] · 2. Some of the time [score: 2] · 3. Often [score: 3]
