## Description

Humans are "social animals" and typically do not like to be on their
own too much. People like to hang out and connect with other
people. People that lack that connection are called lonely .

## Instructions

The statements given below are to identify the way you think and feel.<br> Please read each item carefully and check your response that best describes what you feel and think.<br> Thank you.<br>
